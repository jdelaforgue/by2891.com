I first put by2891.com together in 2019 as a place to show my web work and share the mixes I make in my spare time. It's been due for a refresh for a while, so here it is.

What changed

  • It's faster. No more loading screen, no jQuery or Bootstrap. The whole site is one small page and a little JavaScript.
  • The music keeps playing. Start a mix on the Music page and browse anywhere else on the site without it stopping. It also works with your phone's lock-screen controls.
  • There's a blog. You're reading it.

What's next

I'll use this space to write about building websites, making music, and whatever else is on my mind around Chicago.
